Frequently asked questions

Is this an official FCC or ARRL app?

No. Ham General is an independent study aid. It isn't made by or affiliated with the FCC, the National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators (NCVEC), or the ARRL. Its questions come from the official 2023–2027 General question pool, which NCVEC releases into the public domain. The lessons and explanations are our own.

How closely do the mock exams match the real exam?

The Full Mock is built like the real exam: 35 multiple-choice questions, one from each group of the question pool, with a pass mark of 26. Quick Mock, Smart Practice and the Dynamic Exam let you practice in shorter sessions to the same standard.

Which question pool does the app use?

The 2023–2027 General pool, used for exams from July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2027, including the errata NCVEC published in February 2026. If NCVEC issues further errata, we update the app.

How do I take the real exam?

Exams are given by Volunteer Examiner teams, in person or online. Find a session through a VEC such as the ARRL VEC, or on hamstudy.org. After you pass, the FCC charges an application fee and issues your license. You may transmit as soon as your call sign appears in the FCC's ULS database.

Can I use a calculator in the exam?

Volunteer Examiners generally allow a calculator with its memory cleared, but the VE team has the final say, so ask when you register. A few questions involve a simple calculation, such as Ohm's law or wavelength.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. Everything works offline, there's no sign-up, and the app has no ads or tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress is stored only on your device, so it isn't synced between devices. It's included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup to a new device should bring it with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Profile → Reset all progress and confirm. This clears your XP, streaks, badges, completed lessons and exam history. It can't be undone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
